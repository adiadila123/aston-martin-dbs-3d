Aerodynamic Architecture

Sculpted by the Wind

Harnessing airflow to generate 180 kg of genuine aerodynamic downforce at 211 MPH — the highest figure recorded by an Aston Martin production series grand tourer.

Feature 01

Curlicue Venting

Derived straight from Le Mans prototype racing, deep curlicue vents extract turbulent high-pressure air from the front wheel arches to eradicate front-end lift.

Feature 02

Aeroblade II™

An Aston Martin world first: air is ducted through discreet intakes in the rear C-pillars and channelled through internal ducts to exit as a high-velocity air jet across the deck lid.

Feature 03

Double Diffuser

Accelerates underbody airflow cleanly away from the rear of the car, stabilising high-speed composure through sweeping British and European B-roads.

British Powertrain Engineering

Unbridled Twin-Turbo V12

A 5.2-litre all-alloy quad overhead camshaft 48-valve twin-turbo engine delivering monumental torque from merely 1,800 RPM.

5.2 Litres Engine Architecture Front mid-mounted all-alloy quad-cam 48V twin-turbocharged V12 with water-to-air charge coolers.
715 BHP Power Output Peak power reached at 6,500 RPM, delivering immediate throttle response and a glorious bespoke exhaust note.
ZF 8-Speed Transmission Rear mid-mounted 8-speed automatic with carbon fibre propeller shaft and mechanical limited-slip differential.
410 mm Carbon Ceramic Brakes 6-piston front callipers with ventilated carbon ceramic discs ensuring fade-free deceleration.
3 Drive Modes Dynamic Chassis GT, Sport, and Sport+ calibrations modulating throttle mapping, steering weighting, and adaptive damping.
Quad Exhaust Stainless Steel System Electronically controlled active bypass valves produce an authentic, spine-tingling British soundtrack.
